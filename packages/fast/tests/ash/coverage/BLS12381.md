# BLS12-381 coverage

`BLS12381.mo` imports only `mo:fast/BLS12381` and the corresponding plugin.

- Public group aliases, canonical generators, identity behavior, scalar endianness, addition, MSM and packed aggregation.
- Pairing empty/identity/nonidentity products.
- Independent py_ecc fixtures for IC and Ethereum signatures, wrong messages, PoP validity and wrong-key/domain separation.
- Distinct-message IC aggregate verification and duplicate rejection.
- Ethereum general and same-message aggregate verification with mandatory per-key PoP checks; mismatched proofs fail.
- Empty batches, unequal counts, 128/129 limits, scalar order boundary, decoder/identity error precedence.
- All ten official RFC9380 random-oracle vectors plus the canonical zero-field G2 map exceptional case.

Fixture provenance and reproducible generator live under `plugins/bls12-381/tests/vectors`; official vectors and their provenance live under its `runtime/vectors` and `tests/reference/vectors`. Additional standalone native and portable OCaml regression harnesses cover subgroup/canonical malformed encodings, XMD oversized DSTs, arithmetic edge cases and pairing laws. The independent cross-backend security audit is under `tests/crypto-audit/bls`.

Run `scripts/test-ash.sh BLS12381` after building the plugin.
